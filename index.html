02 · Problem 1

Forward flows are contractive.

Changing the noise barely changes the action, so RL in noise space has almost nothing to explore.

—how much the median noise direction shrinks on its way to an action
Hover to read the share of directions below a stretch · Isaac Lab, Vanilla CFM · all 28 singular values of ∂a/∂z, 100-step flow · 3 training seeds, 50,232 values

03 · Problem 2

The reverse process is expansive.

  • Behaviors are intermixed: nearby noises lead to different action modes.
  • Supervised steering has to learn in an unsmooth latent space.

04 · Idea

Don't let any denoising step contract too much.

LoLip penalizes any denoising step that shrinks its input below a limit.

10 · Core insights

Shape the noise → action map during pretraining.

PROBLEM
Standard flow policies are highly contractive: moving the noise barely moves the action, which hurts both RL and supervised steering in latent space.
CAUSE
Flow matching fits the action distribution but never shapes the noise → action map.
FIX
LoLip lower-bounds the singular values of each denoising step's Jacobian, which lower-bounds the stretch of the whole flow.
RESULT
A less contractive forward map and more stable inversion give better supervised steering (DSBC) and latent-space RL (DSRL).

11 · Limitation

LoLip costs some base performance. DSBC wins it back.

On its own the LoLip base drops from 86% to 70%. DSBC on the pretraining demos brings it to 85%, against 89% for Vanilla.

LIBERO-90, 20 tasks · mean over 3 training seeds, 25 episodes per task · error bars: 1 sd over seeds
